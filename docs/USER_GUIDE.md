# Section 1: End-User Roles

## 1.1 New Doctor Applicant

### Role overview
You are a doctor (or clinic owner) who wants to join the GutGuard partner program. Once you join, you can share your own shop link with patients and earn E-Points on their purchases.

### First-time onboarding

You can start in any of these ways:

- Open **partners.gutguard.ph**. It takes you straight to the application form.
- Scan a partner's **Referral QR** poster, or open a join link like `gutguard.ph/j/10001`. The partner who invited you will get credit.
- In a partner's shop, tap **"Become a partner"** (on phones it says **"Join"**).
- On gutguard.ph, click **"Become a physician partner"** at the bottom of the page.

> **Tip:** If someone invited you, use their link. The system remembers the invitation for 30 days on that browser. If you open the form some other way, nobody gets credit for inviting you.

### Core workflow: Apply and open your dashboard

**Step 1 — Fill in the application form**

You will see a page titled **"Apply to become a partner"**. Fill in:

| Field | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix | Yes | Dr., Dra., Mr., Ms., Mrs., Mx., Prof., or Atty. |
| Name | Yes | Your full name |
| Email address | Yes | You will sign in with this email, so use one you check |
| Mobile | Yes | A PH number such as `09171234567` or `+639171234567` |
| TikTok username | No | Used for your **Profile QR**. Leave it blank if you don't have one |
| Specialty | Yes | If you pick **"Other"**, type your specialty in the extra box |
| Complete Clinic Address | Yes | |
| Where did you find us? | No | For example: colleague, social media, event |

If someone invited you, you will see **"Invited by partner #10xxx"** just above the button. Only the inviting partner's number is shown, not their name.

**Step 2 — Submit**

1. Click **"Submit application"**.
2. The screen shows **"Application received"** and then **"Sending your sign-in code…"**.

**Step 3 — Enter your 6-digit code**

1. You are taken to a **"Check your email"** screen.
2. Open the email with the subject **"Your GutGuard sign-in code"**.
3. Type the 6 digits into **"Verification code"**.
4. Click **"Verify and continue"**.

**Step 4 — You're in**

Your partner dashboard opens. A welcome email (Step 1 of the GutGuard email series) is sent to you.

Your new account is set up like this:

- It starts as an **Affiliate Store**.
- It gets a permanent **partner number** (for example, 10245).
- Your **Shop QR** works right away.
- Your **Referral QR** (for inviting other doctors) stays **locked** until your first customer pays. See section 1.2.

> There is no password. Each time you sign in, you get a new code by email.

### FAQ / Troubleshooting

| Message or problem | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Please complete the highlighted fields." | One or more fields are missing or invalid | Fix every field outlined in red |
| "Please enter a valid PH mobile number." | The number is not `09…` + 9 digits | Re-type it without the country code, e.g. `09171234567` |
| "Enter a valid TikTok username, or leave this blank." | The username has spaces or symbols | Use letters, numbers, `.` or `_` only, or leave it empty |
| "This email has already been registered. Sign in with a one-time code instead." | You already have an account | Click **"Sign in"** and use the same email |
| "This TikTok username has already been registered…" | Another account uses that username | Check the spelling. If it really is yours, contact GutGuard |
| "A partner cannot refer themselves…" | You opened your own invite link | Register without the link, or use a different inviter's link |
| "Your account was created, but we couldn't email a sign-in code." | The account exists but the email failed | Click **"Email me a sign-in code"** on the sign-in screen |
| The "Invited by" line doesn't appear | The invite link was wrong or expired | Ask your inviter to send their link again **before** you submit. Credit can't be added afterwards, except by an admin transfer (see 2.2) |
| I can't find the code email | Spam filter, or a typo in the email | Check your Spam/Promotions folders. Wait 60 seconds, then click **"Resend code"** |

## 1.2 Doctor Partner (Affiliate & Lifestyle Store)

### Role overview
You share GutGuard with patients and colleagues. You use the dashboard to:

- Get your QR codes and links
- See orders from your patients
- Track E-Points and rebate milestones
- See doctors you have invited (Lifestyle only)

There are two store levels at this stage:

| Store level | How you get it | What it can do |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Affiliate** | Every new account starts here | Sell through your **Shop QR**. The Referral QR is locked |
| 🛍️ **Lifestyle** | **Automatic** when your first customer completes a paid order (an admin can also promote you) | Everything above, **plus** the **Referral QR** to invite other doctors |

### Signing in

1. Open **partners.gutguard.ph/partner**. You can also click **"Log in"** on the shop or main site.
2. On **"Welcome back"**, type your **Email address**.
3. Click **"Email me a sign-in code"**.
4. Type the 6-digit code from the email.
5. Click **"Verify and continue"**.

You stay signed in on that browser until you sign out. To sign out, click your name in the top-right corner, then **"Sign out"**.

### Finding your way around

- **Top bar:** **"Copy shop link"** and your name, with your store level (Affiliate / Lifestyle).
- **Left menu** (bottom bar on phones):
  - **Overview** — your summary
  - **Orders** — purchases credited to you
  - **Referred partners** — doctors you invited
  - **E-Points** — points and rebate milestones
  - **Share & QR** — your codes, links and posters
- **Side card "Your shop link"** — shows the number of clicks in the last 30 days.

### Core workflow A: Get your QR codes and share them

1. Click **Share & QR**.
2. Choose a tab:

   | Tab | Link it creates | What happens when someone scans it |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Shop QR** | `gutguard.ph/s/<your number>` | They go to the shop. Their order is credited to you |
   | **Referral QR** | `gutguard.ph/j/<your number>` | They go to the partner application form, with you as the inviter |
   | **Profile QR** | `partners.gutguard.ph/dr/<key>` | They go to your TikTok profile |

3. Use the buttons on the page:
   - **"Copy link"** or **"Open link"** — copy or test the link.
   - **"Copy text"** — copies a ready-made message you can paste into Viber, WhatsApp or SMS.
   - **"Download PNG"** — saves a high-resolution QR image.
   - **"Preview poster"**, then **"Print poster"** — prints an A4 poster.

> **How credit works:** a customer is linked to the **last** partner link they clicked, for **30 days**. If they later click another partner's link, that partner gets the next order.

### Core workflow B: Unlock your Referral QR (Affiliate → Lifestyle)

1. While you are an Affiliate, the tab reads **"Referral QR (Locked)"** and shows **"Referral QR Inactive"**.
2. Share your **Shop QR** with a patient.
3. When that patient's payment is confirmed, your account changes to **Lifestyle** automatically. No action or request is needed.
4. The badge next to your name changes to **"Lifestyle"**, and the Referral QR becomes active.

### Core workflow C: Check your orders

1. Click **Orders**.
2. Pick a tab:
   - **"All orders"**
   - **"Direct"** — orders through your own links
   - **"Referred"** — orders through doctors you invited
3. Filter if needed:
   - **Status:** Paid, Awaiting payment, Delivered, Cancelled, Refunded
   - **Sort**
   - **From / To** dates
   - **"Reset"** clears all filters
4. Click a row to see buyer contact details and the delivery address.

> Buyer contact details are confidential. Use them only to follow up on that order.

### Core workflow D: Track E-Points and rebates

**How points are earned**
- You earn **1 E-Point for every ₱1,000** of a paid order (minimum 1 point per paid order).
- When a doctor you invited makes a sale, you also receive the same number of **pass-up points**. This only goes one level up.
- If an order is refunded or its payment fails, its points are removed.

**Steps**
1. Click **E-Points**.
2. On the **"Rebates"** tab you will see two tracks. Each runs in cycles of 1,500 points:
   - **Track A** — your own shop sales
   - **Track B** — pass-up points from doctors you invited
3. Each track has three milestones:

   | Points in the cycle | Rebate shown |
   |---|---|
   | 300 | ₱18,000 |
   | 750 | ₱70,000 |
   | 1,500 | ₱150,000 |

   A milestone card shows **"✓ Milestone reached"** when you get there.
4. The **"Points Log"** tab lists every point and the order it came from.

> Cash rebates are checked and paid by GutGuard administration. "Milestone reached" means you qualify. It does not mean the money has been sent.

### Core workflow E: View your network (Lifestyle)

1. Click **Referred partners**.
2. **"Team Hierarchy"** lists each doctor you invited, with their orders and the points they passed up to you.
3. **"Partner Directory"** shows the same doctors as cards. Click **"Profile"** for details.

### FAQ / Troubleshooting

| Message or problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This email is not registered as a GutGuard partner." | Sign in with the email you applied with. If GutGuard created your code for you, ask an admin to add your email first |
| "That code isn't correct…" | Re-type the newest code. Each new code replaces the old one |
| "That code has expired…" | Click **"Resend code"** |
| "Too many sign-in emails were sent…" / "A new code can't be sent yet…" | Wait about 5 minutes. Only 2 codes can be sent per email in 5 minutes |
| "Too many sign-in requests…" | Many people on your Wi-Fi are signing in at once. Wait a few minutes |
| My Referral QR is still locked | Your first **paid** order hasn't come through yet. Orders still "Awaiting payment" don't count |
| A patient ordered but it isn't in my list | They may have reached the shop without your link, or clicked someone else's link afterwards. An order is not credited if the buyer's email or mobile matches yours |
| My points and cycle numbers differ between screens | The Overview card adds Track A and Track B together. The E-Points tracks count them separately |
| "Rebate history" is empty even though a milestone is reached | Rebate payouts are recorded by administration. Contact GutGuard to confirm payout |

## 1.3 Main Store Partner

### Role overview
A **Main Store** is a top-level partner that leads its own network of Lifestyle and Affiliate stores. Only a GutGuard admin can upgrade a Lifestyle store to Main Store.

### Signing in
Main Store partners sign in the same way as every partner (section 1.2). Your name badge reads **"Main Store"** in gold.

### What's different
Your menu has these pages:

- **Overview**
- **Reports & Stores**
- **Combined E-Points**
- **Share & QR**

On the Share page, the QR tabs are labelled **"Main Shop QR"** and **"Partner Registration QR"**.

### Core workflow A: Monitor your network
1. **Overview** shows:
   - **Lifestyle stores** and **Affiliate stores** under you
   - **Network orders**
   - **Network gross** (paid orders only)
2. Click **Reports & Stores**.
   - **"Store Directory"** lists every store under you. You can filter by **Type** and sort by points, orders, sales or name. The **Referral QR** column shows each store's QR status (Active / Locked).
   - **"Network Orders"** lists every order across your network. You can filter by **Store** and **Status**. Click **"View"** to see the items and delivery details.

### Core workflow B: Combined E-Points
1. Click **Combined E-Points**.
2. You have **one pooled track**: your own sales plus pass-ups, together, in cycles of 1,500 points. The milestones are the same: 300 / 750 / 1,500 pts → ₱18,000 / ₱70,000 / ₱150,000.

> Pass-up points come only from stores you invited **directly**. Stores further down count toward network orders but do not give you points.

### FAQ / Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Overview tiles all show 0 | Reload the page. If they are still 0, contact GutGuard. Errors on this screen are not shown on the page |
| Search in Network Orders misses an order | Search only covers the page you are looking at. Clear the search and go to the next page |
| Status names differ from the Orders page | "Pending" here is the same as "Awaiting payment", and "Fulfilled" is the same as "Delivered" |

## 1.4 Customer (Shopper)

### Role overview
You want to buy SynBIOTIC+ and pay online. You don't need an account.

### Getting to the shop
- Go to **shop.gutguard.ph**, **or**
- Scan your doctor's **Shop QR** (`gutguard.ph/s/<number>`). The shop will show **"Shop of #<number>"**.

> Links from a doctor often show the **"Try first"** products only. That is normal.

### Core workflow A: Place an order

1. **Choose products.** Under **"Choose your order"**, pick a tab:
   - **"Try first"** — trial packs
   - **"Full protocol"** — Start, Grow or Peak packages

   Then click **"Add <product>"**. If a promo is running, you will see the old price crossed out and a **"Limited time offer"** label.
2. **Review the basket.** The basket opens automatically. Use **−/+** to change the quantity (up to 20 per item). Click **"Checkout"**.
3. **Fill in delivery details.**
   - Email
   - Mobile (`09XX XXX XXXX`)
   - First name and Last name
   - Street address
   - Then choose, in this order: **Capital or Province → City / Municipality → Barangay**
   - The **ZIP** code fills in automatically when it is known.
4. **Check the total.** Shipping is calculated from Davao (J&T Express) as soon as you choose a province.
5. **Pay.** Click **"Pay ₱<total> with Maya"**.
   - The screen shows **"Order <code> saved."** Your order is saved **before** payment, so it won't be lost.
   - You are taken to Maya's secure page. You can pay by card, Maya wallet, QRPh or online banking.
6. **Come back.** After payment you return to your order page automatically.

### Core workflow B: Track your order

- Open the link in the email **"We received your GutGuard order GG-…"**, **or** go to `shop.gutguard.ph/shop/order/<your order code>`.
- Order codes look like `GG-20261026-1A2B3C4D`.

| Status on page | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | The order is saved but not paid | Click **"Pay ₱… with Maya"** |
| **Confirming** | Maya is still confirming | Wait. The page updates by itself within about 20 seconds |
| **Paid** | Payment received | Nothing. GutGuard will call you to confirm delivery before shipping |
| **Payment on hold** | The payment went through and is being verified | Nothing. You'll be emailed once it clears |
| **Payment failed** | Nothing was charged | Try again with the same or a different payment method |
| **Cancelled at Maya** | You stopped the payment | Pay whenever you're ready |
| **Cancelled** | The payment window expired | Place a new order |
| **Refunded** | Money returned | Allow a few banking days |

You will receive two emails:
1. **Order received** — sent when your order is saved.
2. **"Payment confirmed for GutGuard order …"** — sent once payment clears.

### FAQ / Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Address lists could not be loaded…" | Check your internet and click **"Try again"** |
| "We could not open the Maya checkout just now, but nothing was lost." | Click **"Try payment again"**, or **"Open my order page"** and pay later |
| "Your delivery address could not be verified…" | Re-select province, city and barangay from the lists, then order again |
| "Order totals could not be verified…" | Prices changed since you added items. Empty the basket and add the items again |
| "We can't price shipping to this province…" / "This order is above 6kg…" | Message GutGuard on Messenger (chat bubble, bottom corner) before paying |
| An old Maya payment link doesn't work | Maya sessions expire after about an hour. Always pay from your **order page** |
| Wrong address after paying | Contact GutGuard with your order code **before** it ships |

## 1.5 Story Contributor (Testimonials)

### Role overview
You are a GutGuard user who wants to share your experience on the **Stories** page (`gutguard.ph/testimonials`).

### Access
No account is needed. Go to **Stories** and click **"Share your story →"**. You can also open `/testimonials/share` directly.

### Core workflow: Submit a story

1. Fill in:
   - **Your name** (2–80 characters)
   - **Email** (never published)
   - **City or role** (optional)
   - **Your story** (40–1,500 characters)
2. Add pictures if you like:
   - **Profile photo**: one image
   - **Photos**: up to 4. JPG, PNG or WebP, 10 MB each
3. **Video (optional).** Videos can't be uploaded directly. Instead:
   1. Upload the video to Google Drive.
   2. In Drive, open **Share → General access** and choose **"Anyone with the link"**.
   3. Paste that link into **Video link**.
4. Tick the **consent** box.
5. Click **"Send my story"**.
6. You will see **"Thank you — we have it."** A GutGuard staff member reads every story before it is published. This usually takes a few days.

### FAQ / Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That does not look like a Google Drive link…" | Copy the share link from Google Drive itself |
| "You already have stories awaiting review…" | You can have up to 3 stories waiting at once. Wait a day |
| My video won't play on the site | The Drive file isn't public. Set **"Anyone with the link"** |
| My story was not published | Stories that claim to treat, cure or prevent a disease, or that name another person without their permission, cannot be published |

# Section 2: Administrator Guide

### Role overview
Administrators are GutGuard staff. They:

- Manage partners and store levels
- Handle orders and payments
- Set prices and promos
- Send campaigns
- Moderate stories

All admin work is done at **`/admin`**, the **Operations CRM**.

### Signing in

1. Open `partners.gutguard.ph/admin`.
2. Enter the **Administrator Password**.
3. Click **"Unlock CRM Console"**.

Things to know about admin sessions:

- Sessions last **8 hours**. To sign out early, click **"Sign Out"**, at the top right or in the sidebar.
- After **8 failed attempts in 15 minutes** from one network, login is blocked. The message is: "Too many login attempts. Try again in a few minutes."
- If a page suddenly shows **"Unauthorized: Admin session expired."**, reload the page and sign in again.

### The sidebar

| Group | Pages |
|---|---|
| Overview | Dashboard |
| Commerce & Sales | Shop Orders, Product Catalog, Promotions, Referred Clinics, TikTok Shop |
| Partner Network | Doctor Partners, Partner Codes, Referral Email |
| Marketing & Automation | Wheel Prizes, Newsletter, SMS Blast, Email Sequence |
| Content & Moderation | Testimonials |

## 2.1 System Configuration

### Global rules (all staff and all AI tools)
- **Production is controlled by atc1989 only.** Nobody else may:
  - push or merge to the `main` branch
  - change Vercel production settings
  - run SQL against the `doctors` (production) database schema
- Every change goes to **`sandbox`** first. The sandbox site is `sandbox.gutguard.ph`, and it uses the `sandbox` database schema.
- **Never paste passwords, keys or tokens into the code repository.** The repository is public.

### Where settings live

| Setting | Where it is changed | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Admin password, Maya payment keys, site addresses, database schema | Vercel → Project → Environment Variables | atc1989 (production) |
| Email sending (Resend), SMS gateway (iTexMo), TikTok Shop keys | Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets | atc1989 |
| Sign-in email template and sender | Supabase → Authentication → Email Templates / SMTP | atc1989 |
| Products, prices, promos | **Admin → Product Catalog / Promotions** | Any admin |
| Referral email, email sequence | **Admin → Referral Email / Email Sequence** | Any admin |

### Security settings to keep correct

- **Admin password.**
  - There is one shared password. It must be identical in two places: Vercel (`ADMIN_PASSWORD`) and the database admin settings.
  - Changing it signs out every admin.
- **Rate limiting (Upstash).**
  - These settings must be present in production. Without them, sign-in and login limits are switched off and "Sign Out" doesn't fully end a session.
- **Maya.**
  - Production must use the **live** Maya address (`pg.paymaya.com`) with live keys.
  - Sandbox keys in production would mark test-card orders as paid.
- **Sign-in emails.**
  - The Supabase "Magic Link" email template must include the 6-digit code.
  - Email must be sent through Resend. The built-in sender is limited to about 30 emails per hour, which makes partner sign-in look broken.

### Managing products — Admin → Product Catalog

1. Choose a tab:
   - **Active** — in the shop
   - **Hidden** — not in the shop, but can still be used in promos
   - **Archived** — retired
2. Edit the fields: **Price**, **Price per capsule**, **Capsules**, **Display order** and **Status**, plus the phase and dosage text.
3. Click **"Save"**. If you changed a price, confirm the pop-up. Customers with unpaid orders at the old price will be asked to rebuild their basket.

> Products can't be added or deleted here. To remove a product from sale, set it to **Hidden** or **Archived**.

### Managing promotions — Admin → Promotions

1. Click **"New promo"**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Promo name**
   - Optional **Shop label**
   - **Starts** and **Ends** (leave Ends blank for no end date)
3. Tick the products and set a **Discount** (1–90%). You can also type a final price.
4. Tick **"Promo is on"**, then click **"Save promo"**.

Rules to know:

- Dates are Philippine time. A promo ends at 11:59 PM on its end date.
- Overlapping promos **don't add up**. Each product gets the biggest discount.
- **"Switch off"** applies immediately, with no confirmation.
- **"Delete"** asks for confirmation.

## 2.2 User Management

### Accounts and approval
- **Partners are approved automatically.** No one needs to approve a sign-up. Every application becomes an **Affiliate Store** straight away.
- **Admins** share one password. There are no separate admin user accounts, so actions can't be traced to a specific person.

### Find a partner — Admin → Doctor Partners

1. Use the filters:
   - **All / Direct / Referred**
   - **Store Type**
   - **Search** — name, email, partner number, referrer, clinic, or the word "pending"
2. Click a row to see the details:
   - Contact details
   - Specialty and clinic address
   - **Origin**: "Referred by X" or "Direct registration"
   - **Where found**

### Change a partner's store level

| Action | Button | Available for | Confirmation? | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Promote | **"Promote to Lifestyle"** | Affiliate | Yes | Becomes Lifestyle, and the Referral QR turns on |
| Upgrade | **"⬆ Upgrade to Main"** | Lifestyle | Yes, with a "Breakaway Impact" warning | Becomes a Main Store. Cuts the link to the old upline. Its whole downline moves under it |
| Referral QR | **"Enable QR" / "Disable QR"** | Affiliate | **No** | Turns the inviting QR on or off |
| Shop link | **"Show Full Protocol in shop link"** | All | **No** | Off = the partner's link shows "Try first" only. On = shows everything |

> **Breakaway can't be undone from the screen.** Read the impact list before you confirm.

### Fix who invited a partner — "Transfer"

Use this when an Affiliate store was registered under the wrong inviter, or with no inviter.

1. Find the store and click **"Transfer"**.
2. Choose the **New upline**. Optionally add an **Admin Note**.
3. Click **"Confirm Transfer"**.
4. The store moves under the new upline's Main Store. Any pass-up points already earned move to the new upline too.

Only **Affiliate** stores can be transferred.

### Give "unclaimed" pass-up points

Stores with no inviter show **"{n} unclaimed pass-up pts"**.

1. Click **"Give points"**.
2. Choose who should receive the points.
3. Click **"Give Points"**.

### Create a code for a doctor who hasn't registered yet — Admin → Partner Codes

1. Click **"Generate code"**.
2. Check the account number and links in the confirmation pop-up.
3. Click **"Generate code"** again to confirm.
4. Download the **Shop** and **Join** QR images (**"Download PNG"**) and give them to the doctor.
5. The code starts working from the first scan. It shows as **Pending** until you finish the next step.
6. Let the doctor sign in:
   1. Go to **Doctor Partners** and search for the code.
   2. Click **"Edit"**.
   3. Fill in their name and **email**.
   4. Click **"Save changes"**.

### Credentials and sign-in problems
Partners have **no passwords**, so there is nothing to reset. When a partner can't sign in:

1. **Check the email.** In **Doctor Partners → Edit**, confirm the email on file matches the one they are typing. Correct it and click **"Save changes"**.
2. **Check that the email was received.** Ask them to look in Spam, wait 5 minutes, and request a new code.
3. **See what they see.** Click **"Log in as"** and confirm the pop-up.
   - Their dashboard opens in a new tab.
   - Every use of "Log in as" is recorded in the impersonation log.
   - Using it replaces any partner session already open in your browser.

### Handling escalations

| Partner complaint | Check | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "My patient's order isn't credited to me" | **Shop Orders** → open the order → **Came from** | Shows "Direct" = the customer didn't use the partner's link. Shows "(not credited - self-referral)" = the buyer's email or mobile matches the partner's. The screen has no way to re-credit an order. Escalate to atc1989 for a committed data fix |
| "I was invited by Dr. X but it shows someone else" | **Doctor Partners** → Origin | **Transfer** (Affiliate stores only) |
| "My Referral QR is still locked" | Do they have a paid order? | Wait for the first paid order, or **Promote to Lifestyle** |
| "Where is my rebate?" | E-Points milestones | The system shows when a milestone is reached but **doesn't record payouts**. Handle payout outside the system and keep your own records |

## 2.3 Data & Monitoring

### Dashboard — Admin → Dashboard
- Summary cards: **Doctor Partners**, **Shop Orders**, **Catalog & Promos**, **Stories & Reviews** (the number of stories waiting).
- A card showing "—" means its data didn't load. Reload the page.

### Orders and payments — Admin → Shop Orders

1. Search by order code, name, email, mobile, city, Maya reference or partner.
2. Click **"View"** to open **Order Detail**. It shows:
   - Items
   - Shipping
   - Maya payment ID and reference
   - Payment attempts
   - Which partner gets credit
3. Daily routine for **Paid** orders:
   1. Call the customer.
   2. Set **Order status** to **Confirmed**.
   3. After shipping, set it to **Fulfilled**.
   4. Record call or shipping notes in **Admin notes**.
   5. Click **"Save order"**.

> **Status changes save without a confirmation pop-up, and they affect partner points:**
> - Setting **Payment status = Paid** gives E-Points to the partner and their upline. A first paid order also promotes an Affiliate to Lifestyle.
> - Changing a Paid order to anything else **removes** those points.

**Refunds**
- There is **no refund button**. Make the refund **inside the Maya Business dashboard**.
- Maya then tells the system, and the order becomes **Cancelled / Refunded** by itself.
- Setting "Refunded" by hand in the CRM only changes the label and removes points. **It does not return any money.**

**A payment Maya didn't report**
- Most payments update by themselves. When a customer opens their order page, it also double-checks with Maya.
- If a payment still shows unpaid but Maya shows it as successful:
  1. Copy the **Maya reference** into the order.
  2. Set **Payment status = Paid**.
  3. Save.

## 2.4 Maintenance & Troubleshooting

### Common admin errors

| Message | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Invalid admin password." when the password is right | The password is missing or different on the server | Ask atc1989 to check `ADMIN_PASSWORD` in Vercel |
| "Too many login attempts…" | 8 failed tries in 15 minutes | Wait 15 minutes |
| "Admin login is temporarily unavailable…" | The rate-limit service (Upstash) is down | Try again shortly. Report it if it keeps happening |
| "Unauthorized: Admin session expired." | 8 hours have passed, or the password changed | Reload and sign in |
| "Supabase is not configured." / "…service role credentials are not configured." | Server settings are missing | Escalate to atc1989 |
| "Missing Edge Function secrets" | Email or SMS keys are not set in Supabase | Escalate to atc1989 |
| "SMS provider is not configured…" | No SMS gateway is set | Escalate. SMS can't be sent until the gateway is set up |
| "Only Affiliate stores can be transferred…" | You tried to transfer a Lifestyle or Main store | Only Affiliate stores can be moved |
| "Can only upgrade a Lifestyle Store to Main Store…" | The store isn't Lifestyle | Promote it to Lifestyle first |
| "This doctor has no email address…" (Log in as) | It is a Pending code | Add their email via **Edit** |
| Customer: "Payments are not configured on this environment." | Maya keys are missing | Escalate to atc1989 urgently: no one can pay |

### Database changes and data fixes

1. Every change to the database structure is a migration file in `supabase/migrations/`. It runs on **sandbox** first.
2. Data corrections (`UPDATE` / `INSERT` / `DELETE`) are also saved as `.sql` files and run on sandbox first. **No one-off edits in production.**
3. Anything destructive (`DROP`, `TRUNCATE`, or `DELETE` without a filter) needs atc1989's approval in chat.
4. Add every new migration to the checklist in `RELEASE.md`. Production does not keep its own record of which migrations ran.
5. Only atc1989 applies changes to production.

### Backups

1. **Before every production release or data fix**, atc1989 takes a backup: Supabase → **Database → Backups**.
2. To restore, use the same screen. This affects the whole database, so it is done by atc1989 only.
3. Product prices, promos and email templates are stored in the database. A backup covers them too.

### Releasing (atc1989 only)
Follow `RELEASE.md` step by step:

1. Check settings.
2. Take a backup.
3. Run the migrations.
4. Deploy.
5. Redeploy the edge functions.
6. Smoke test: register a test doctor, sign in as a partner, check the admin doctor list, and make one real Maya payment.

# Appendix

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Affiliate Store** | A new partner level. Can sell, can't invite other doctors yet |
| **Lifestyle Store** | Unlocked by the first paid sale. Can invite doctors |
| **Main Store** | Top of a network. Set by an admin through "Upgrade to Main" |
| **Upline** | The partner who invited you |
| **Downline** | Partners you invited |
| **Partner number / code** | A permanent number starting at 10001, used in `/s/` and `/j/` links |
| **E-Point** | 1 point per ₱1,000 of a paid order |
| **Pass-up points** | Points your upline receives when you sell (one level only) |
| **Cycle** | Every 1,500 points. Milestones restart each cycle |
| **Breakaway** | A Lifestyle store becoming a Main Store and taking its downline with it |
| **Sandbox** | The test copy of the system (`sandbox.gutguard.ph`) |

## Known limitations (as of 2026-10-26)

These come from the current code. Staff should know about them before answering partner questions.

1. **Rebate payouts aren't recorded by the system.** "Milestone reached" is calculated from points, but nothing creates a payout record. Partners' "Rebate history" stays empty.
2. **Refunds must be made in Maya.** The CRM's "Refunded" status doesn't move money.
3. **Audit logs aren't viewable in the CRM**, and they can't tell admins apart (one shared password).
4. **Editing a partner's "Routing slug" in Doctor Partners → Edit has no effect.** The field is not saved.
5. **The Referral QR lock only hides the QR code.** Links an Affiliate already shared still register new doctors under them.
6. **"Prize" in partner details always shows "Not spun".** The prize wheel is no longer part of registration.
7. **The program PDFs in the site files** (onboarding, compensation, consent, FAQ, dosing guide) are not linked from any page. Share them directly if needed.
8. **The Overview and E-Points pages count cycles differently.** Overview adds Track A and Track B together; E-Points counts them separately.
9. **No email is sent for failed, cancelled or shipped orders.** Only "order received" and "payment confirmed" emails exist.
