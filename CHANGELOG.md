# Changelog

User-visible changes only. Rules: see "Patch notes" in `AGENTS.md`.

## Unreleased
- Admin has a new, simpler look for the sign-in page and the frame around every page: a light gray side menu with shorter section names (Orders, Products, Doctor partners…), a top bar with the page name and a "Sandbox" tag on the test site, and Live shop and Sign out at the bottom of the menu. The Dashboard now shows four totals (doctor partners, orders, products, stories to review) and a short "Needs attention" list (payments to review, orders to ship, withdrawal requests, stories to review); staff logins only see the parts they can open. Orders has the new look too: totals for payments to review, to ship, revenue and orders this month; tabs for All, Payment review, To ship and Fulfilled; and a table where every column can be sorted, filtered by where the order came from or payment method, searched, shown or hidden, and exported to a CSV file (Excel opens it). Clicking an order opens it in a side panel (customer, payment, items with totals, status, notes and fulfilment), and Add order uses the same panel with the total on the save button; "Log in as partner" now asks for confirmation inside the panel. Products is now a short table (price with any live promo price, per capsule, capsules, status) with Active, Hidden and Archived tabs; clicking a product opens its settings in a side panel, and changing the price shows a warning before you save instead of a pop-up. Promotions is now a table (dates, discounted products, status) with All, Live, Scheduled, Ended and Off tabs and a ⋯ menu to switch a promo off or delete it; New promo and clicking a promo open a side panel with calendar date pickers, a tick box and discount, final price and per-capsule fields per product, and an on/off switch; deleting asks in a small confirmation window. Referred clinics shows totals (shops, joined by referral, orders through shop links, paid sales), All / Referred / Direct tabs, and the same searchable, sortable, filterable table with CSV export (store type and referrer are filter buttons; "Group by referrer" is replaced by the referrer filter); clicking a shop shows its contact, network and sales. Withdrawals shows totals (pending with the amount to send, approved, rejected, paid out), Pending / Approved / Rejected / All tabs and the shared table with a GCash / Maya / Bank filter and CSV export; Approve and Reject open a small window that says exactly how much to send and where, and asks for the transfer reference or the reason; clicking a request shows its details with a Copy button for the account number. Partner codes lists pending codes in a searchable table (code, description, where it's used, created); Generate code opens a small window with the account number and link preview and the two optional fields, then shows the new code's Shop and Join QRs in a side panel with Share and Download (PNG) buttons. Doctor partners is now a table instead of tall cards: totals (partners, joined by referral, affiliates, unclaimed pass-up points), All / Referred / Direct tabs, store-type and referrer filters, search and CSV export; each partner's ⋯ menu shows only the actions that apply (edit, log in as, upgrade to Main, referral QR on/off, promote, transfer, give unclaimed points), each confirmed in a small window with a searchable partner picker; the edit panel shows the Shop and Join QRs with the "Show Full Protocol" switch, and changes to the routing slug are now actually saved (before, they were silently dropped). Referral email shows the form next to a live preview of the email with sample names filled in, has a Send automatically switch, placeholders you click to copy, and Save / Send test at the bottom (Send test says "Save and send test" when there are unsaved changes); errors now show in red. Testimonials shows totals (to review, published, whose story is featured, rejected), To review / Published / Rejected / All tabs and a searchable table with each story's first line, media and status; reviewing a story opens a side panel with the full story, photos (click for full size) and video, a reminder to play the video first, and a note when featuring will replace the current featured story. Wheel prizes shows totals (prizes on the wheel, prizes left and given out, out of stock, the most likely prize) and a short table with each prize in its own wheel colours, its odds, stock and status; the ⋯ menu edits a prize or turns it off and on, and New prize and Edit open a side panel with GutGuard colour swatches (or any colour), weight, stock and wheel order, and the odds the prize will have once saved. The page now shows each prize's real weight, stock, order and text colour (before, it showed defaults, and saving could overwrite the real stock). Newsletter has Compose and Past sends tabs: Compose shows the subject, an Upload HTML button and click-to-copy placeholders (ticked when the HTML uses them) next to a live preview with sample names, and a recipients table where you tick partners (the top box ticks everyone matching the search, on every page), see each one's last newsletter, and after sending see whether theirs was sent, failed or skipped; Review and send opens a small window listing every address first. Past sends lists each newsletter with its sent, failed and skipped counts, and clicking one shows who got it. SMS blast works the same way: a campaign name only admins see, a message box that counts characters and SMS per partner (and says when emoji drop the limit to 70), placeholders that drop in where the cursor is, a phone-style preview with sample names, a note that sending needs an SMS gateway on the server, the same tickable recipients table (only partners with a valid PH mobile, with a count of those left out), a confirm window with the total SMS and every number, and Past sends. Email sequence shows totals (enrolled, in progress, finished, clicked a step) and two tabs: Steps lists the emails in order with how many partners are on each, can be reordered by dragging or with Move up / Move down, and opens a side panel with the subject, HTML upload, a live preview, click-to-copy placeholders and attachments (with a warning when they get large); deleting a step now asks first and says how many partners are on it. Partners lists everyone enrolled with their step, status and last click (partners left on a step that was deleted are marked), loads straight away instead of only after Refresh, and resending or sending the next step asks first. The old Registration Email screen is gone. Orders, Products, Promotions, Referred clinics, Withdrawals, Partner codes, Doctor partners, Testimonials and Wheel prizes rows have a ⋯ menu (Edit, plus Log in as partner on referred orders, Hide from shop / Show in shop on products); clicking a row still opens it. Side panels, menus and pop-ups slide and fade open and closed, and the side menu can be collapsed to icons with « (names show on hover; the choice is remembered). On these new pages, dropdowns, filter menus and date fields use the admin's own simple style (a calendar with a typed time instead of the browser's date box, a searchable partner list), nothing in a list is pre-highlighted when it opens, and the focus outline is a thin gray line. Tables can show 10, 20, 50 or 100 rows per page. Every Admin page now has the new look.
- Admin: new Staff Accounts page (Settings → Staff Accounts, super admin only) where the super admin creates staff logins (username and password) and ticks the sections each one can open, in the admin's new look: a table of accounts with what each can open and whether it's on, a side panel to create or edit one, a ⋯ menu to turn an account off or delete it (deleting asks first); staff only see those sections, and only the super admin can "Log in as" a partner. The admin login now asks for a username (super admin: "admin"), and everyone is signed out of Admin once when this goes live.
- Admin email settings, email sequences, newsletters, SMS blasts, "log in as doctor" and paid order receipts work again (they had stopped with server errors after Supabase began giving the edge functions a different service key).
- Shop payments: retries check earlier payments and reuse active checkout sessions, payment recovery survives failed saves, and missing payment receipts can be retried without charging again.
- TikTok is removed everywhere: no TikTok username on the registration form or in Admin → Doctors, no Profile QR (old /dr/ links now open the home page), no Admin → TikTok Shop page, and the welcome and newsletter emails describe the GutGuard Partner Program instead of the TikTok Affiliate Program.
- Partner dashboard: new Wallet page. Partners earn 10% of the products (not shipping) on every paid order through their own shop link, and can convert direct G-Points into the wallet (300 pts = ₱18,000, 750 = ₱70,000, 1,500 = ₱150,000; only the converted points are used up, and G-Points Track A now shows the points left to convert). The page shows available, pending, withdrawn and total earned, an earnings list, a withdrawal request form (GCash, Maya or bank; minimum ₱500, fixed ₱60 fee) and withdrawal history with status. Admin → Withdrawals lists requests; admins approve with the transfer reference or reject with a reason (the amount returns to the partner's wallet).
- Partner QR codes (Share page, Admin → Partner Codes and Admin → Doctors) now have the GutGuard logo in the middle, on screen and in the downloaded PNG.
- "Copy link" buttons are now "Share link": on phones they open the share menu, elsewhere they copy the link.
- Points are now called G-Points everywhere in the partner dashboard.
- Partner overview (Affiliate): the welcome banner is blue and can be closed with an X (it stays closed); the "Referred-partner orders" tile is replaced by a blue "Share link" tile; the shop link card explains that the first paid order unlocks the Referral QR.
- Shop: the Full protocol tab is marked with a blue "Recommended" badge.
- Registration form: the "Where did you find us?" example now reads "e.g. Friend, Facebook, clinic event".
- Admin: Affiliate stores can be transferred to a new upline from the Doctors list (the pass-up points they already earned move to the new upline), and pass-up points from stores with no upline show on the store's row and can be given to a partner.
- Partner codes: every partner now has a short number (e.g. 10001) and their QR codes link to `gutguard.ph/s/<number>` for the shop and `gutguard.ph/j/<number>` to join; admins can create a code with both QRs for a doctor before they register (Admin → Partner Codes, with a confirmation showing the account number first), the shop header shows "Become a partner" instead of Log in (in a partner's shop it also says "Shop of #<number>"; if that partner can't recruit yet, it shows Log in instead), and the registration form shows "Ref number #<number>" above Submit instead of the inviter's initials. When generating a code, admins can add a public label (e.g. "GSC", shown beside the ref number on the registration form: "Ref number #10341 · GSC"), an admin-only description (shown in the pending list) and where the QR will be used, which is pre-filled as "Where did you find us?" for doctors who join through it. All three can be changed later on Doctor partners (in the partner's edit panel), before or after the doctor signs up.
- Admin: every admin page now shares one sidebar navigation and sign-in, with a new admin home page; Orders opens on its own page again.
- Admin → Orders: "Add order" records a Consignment or Cash sale (customer, address, products, payment reference and an optional referring partner) and saves it as paid straight away, with catalog prices and no shipping fee; partner G-Points and the receipt email follow. Shop checkout stays Maya only. Add order also accepts Maya. The order list has a Payment column showing the method (Maya, Consignment, Cash) and whether the sale was Online (shop checkout) or Store (Add order). Every order now has fulfilment fields - order time, POF time, tracking number, pick-up time, tracking status (Preparing → Delivered / Returned), bot message and salesperson - filled in Add order and editable from the order's View dialog. If Add order fails partway, the order is cancelled instead of left paid, so trying again never creates a second paid order.
- Admin → Wheel: downloading a doctor's QR now saves a PNG image instead of an SVG.
