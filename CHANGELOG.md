# Changelog

User-visible changes only. Rules: see "Patch notes" in `AGENTS.md`.

## Unreleased
- Admin has a new, simpler look for the sign-in page and the frame around every page: a light gray side menu with shorter section names (Orders, Products, Doctor partners…), a top bar with the page name and a "Sandbox" tag on the test site, and Live shop and Sign out at the bottom of the menu. The Dashboard now shows four totals (doctor partners, orders, products, stories to review) and a short "Needs attention" list (payments to review, orders to ship, withdrawal requests, stories to review); staff logins only see the parts they can open. Orders has the new look too: totals for payments to review, to ship, revenue and orders this month; tabs for All, Payment review, To ship and Fulfilled; and a table where every column can be sorted, filtered by where the order came from or payment method, searched, shown or hidden, and exported to a CSV file (Excel opens it). Clicking an order still opens the old detail window for now. Products is now a short table (price with any live promo price, per capsule, capsules, status) with Active, Hidden and Archived tabs; clicking a product opens its settings in a side panel, and changing the price shows a warning before you save instead of a pop-up. The other pages keep their old look for now.
- Admin: staff can get their own login that only opens the sections they are given (for example Orders and Products); the menu shows only those sections, and only the owner login can "Log in as" a partner. Everyone is signed out of Admin once when this goes live.
- Admin email settings, email sequences, newsletters, SMS blasts, "log in as doctor" and paid order receipts work again (they had stopped with server errors after Supabase began giving the edge functions a different service key).
- Shop payments: retries check earlier payments and reuse active checkout sessions, payment recovery survives failed saves, and missing payment receipts can be retried without charging again.
- TikTok is removed everywhere: no TikTok username on the registration form or in Admin → Doctors, no Profile QR (old /dr/ links now open the home page), no Admin → TikTok Shop page, and the welcome and newsletter emails describe the GutGuard Partner Program instead of the TikTok Affiliate Program.
- Partner dashboard: new Wallet page. Partners earn 10% of the products (not shipping) on every paid order through their own shop link, and can convert direct G-Points into the wallet (300 pts = ₱18,000, 750 = ₱70,000, 1,500 = ₱150,000; only the converted points are used up, and G-Points Track A now shows the points left to convert). The page shows available, pending, withdrawn and total earned, an earnings list, a withdrawal request form (GCash, Maya or bank; minimum ₱500, fixed ₱60 fee) and withdrawal history with status. Admin → Withdrawals lists requests; admins approve with the transfer reference or reject with a reason (the amount returns to the partner's wallet).
- Partner QR codes (Share page, Admin → Partner Codes and Admin → Doctors) now have the GutGuard logo in the middle, on screen and in the downloaded PNG.
- "Copy link" buttons are now "Share link": on phones they open the share menu, elsewhere they copy the link.
- Points are now called G-Points everywhere in the partner dashboard.
- Partner overview (Affiliate): the welcome banner is blue and can be closed with an X (it stays closed); the "Referred-partner orders" tile is replaced by a blue "Share link" tile; the shop link card explains that the first paid order unlocks the Referral QR.
- Shop: the Full protocol tab is marked with a blue "Recommended" badge.
- Registration form: the "Where did you find us?" example now reads "e.g. Friend, Facebook, clinic event".
- Admin: Affiliate stores can be transferred to a new upline from the Doctors list (the pass-up points they already earned move to the new upline), and pass-up points from stores with no upline show on the store's row and can be given to a partner.
- Partner codes: every partner now has a short number (e.g. 10001) and their QR codes link to `gutguard.ph/s/<number>` for the shop and `gutguard.ph/j/<number>` to join; admins can create a code with both QRs for a doctor before they register (Admin → Partner Codes, with a confirmation showing the account number first), the shop header shows "Become a partner" instead of Log in (in a partner's shop it also says "Shop of #<number>"; if that partner can't recruit yet, it shows Log in instead), and the registration form shows "Ref number #<number>" above Submit instead of the inviter's initials. When generating a code, admins can add a description (shown in the pending list) and where the QR will be used, which is pre-filled as "Where did you find us?" for doctors who join through it.
- Admin: every admin page now shares one sidebar navigation and sign-in, with a new admin home page; Orders opens on its own page again.
- Admin → Orders: "Add order" records a Consignment or Cash sale (customer, address, products, payment reference and an optional referring partner) and saves it as paid straight away, with catalog prices and no shipping fee; partner G-Points and the receipt email follow. Shop checkout stays Maya only. Add order also accepts Maya. The order list has a Payment column showing the method (Maya, Consignment, Cash) and whether the sale was Online (shop checkout) or Store (Add order). Every order now has fulfilment fields - order time, POF time, tracking number, pick-up time, tracking status (Preparing → Delivered / Returned), bot message and salesperson - filled in Add order and editable from the order's View dialog. If Add order fails partway, the order is cancelled instead of left paid, so trying again never creates a second paid order.
- Admin → Wheel: downloading a doctor's QR now saves a PNG image instead of an SVG.
